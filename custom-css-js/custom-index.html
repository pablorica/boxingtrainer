Welcome to Boxing Trainer London – Expert Personal Training and World-Class Boxing Coaching in London.

As one of London’s top personal trainers and an undefeated British University Heavyweight Boxing Champion, Paul Carroll combines elite boxing expertise with a diverse athletic background. His sports achievements include being a champion swimmer, a junior international decathlete for Team GB, and a skilled competitor in multiple team sports.

With a BSc Honours degree in Sport Science, Paul has extensive qualifications as a head personal trainer, industry assessor, and master trainer. He also writes expert blogs on fitness, boxing techniques, and personal training to help you stay informed and motivated. Read the latest blog posts here.

At Boxing Trainer London, Paul provides customised personal training and boxing coaching in London, tailored to your specific needs. Whether you’re a beginner or an advanced athlete refining your technique, Paul’s varied sessions are designed to achieve your fitness goals in a motivating, professional atmosphere.
